# About

<p class="lede">Architect, Integration at <a href="https://wso2.com">WSO2</a> and compiler developer for <a href="https://ballerina.io">Ballerina</a>.</p>

<!-- social -->

I have been at WSO2 for more than 13 years. Today I lead the design of [Durable Workflow](/projects/#durable-workflow) in WSO2 Integrator, and I started [Agent-Flavored Markdown (AFM)](/projects/#afm), an open specification for portable AI agents written in Markdown.

Before this, I led the design and architecture of the low-code editor that became [WSO2 Integrator](/projects/#wso2-integrator). I have worked on the [Ballerina compiler and language specification](/projects/#ballerina-compiler) since 2016. My first years at WSO2 were on the [Business Process Server](/projects/#wso2-bps), with BPMN, WS-BPEL and WS-HumanTask.

BSc (Hons) in Computer Science and Engineering, University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka.

Outside work, I play Dota 2.
